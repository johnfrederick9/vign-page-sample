The idea in one line

Companies need a property or a store checked. VIGN has inspectors and mystery shoppers across the US. The system connects them, from the order all the way to the report and the payment.

The screens you have seen so far are a preview with sample data. Names, addresses, prices and scores are made up, so we can agree on how everything looks and works before the real system goes live.

1Who uses the system

Website visitorsAnyone. They read about your services, send an inspection request, or sign up as an inspector, free.
You (Admin)Your own page. You see everything: new requests, jobs, inspectors, reports, invoices, payments and documents.
Your office staffThe daily work: enter jobs, assign them, review reports, send invoices, pay inspectors.
Inspectors and shoppersUse their phone: accept jobs, fill in the checklist on site, take photos, see their pay.
ClientsBanks, insurers, stores and food chains. They order inspections, follow them, read reports and pay invoices.

Everyone sees only their own things. Inspectors and clients never see your admin page, and one client never sees another client's jobs.

2How people join

A new inspector
  1. Signs up free on the website
  2. You see the sign-up in Approve New Inspectors
  3. You approve and set their area: ZIP codes, counties or a whole state
  4. They start getting job offers for that area
A new client
  1. Sends a first request from the website form, no account needed
  2. Your office sets them up with a billing plan: per job, monthly or yearly
  3. They get their own login and sign the agreement online
  4. From then on they order, follow jobs and see reports in their portal

3The life of one job

Client orders → You accept → Offered to inspectors → First to accept gets it → Visit and report → Your office checks it → Client sees it → Invoice → Inspector paid

  1. The order comes in Client

    From the website, the client's portal, or a spreadsheet with many jobs at once (for example all of a chain's stores). It shows up in your New Inspection Requests.

  2. You accept the job You

    One click on Accept Job. Every job gets a due date of 10 days at most. Jobs that happen once or twice a month can be set to repeat automatically.

  3. It goes out to the area You

    The job is offered to all available inspectors who cover that area. Or you can pick one inspector yourself.

  4. First to accept gets it Inspector

    Inspectors see the offer on their phone with Accept or Decline. When you snooze, you lose: the first one to accept gets the job. If it's declined, it comes back to you to give to someone else.

  5. The visit and the report Inspector

    On site, the inspector rates each item on the checklist, takes photos and sends the report from the phone. If the job is still open on day 5, the inspector gets an automatic reminder email.

  6. Your office checks it You Staff

    Every report is checked before the client sees it. Approve it, or send it back to the inspector with a note to fix it.

  7. The client sees the result Client

    The client sees the grade, the checklist and the photos. Their locations are ranked from the highest grade to the lowest, so they see their best performing stores first.

4How the money works

From clients
  • Billed per job, or by a monthly or yearly plan
  • Billing runs automatically
  • A card is charged automatically only when the client has signed the agreement
  • You always see which invoices are open, due and past due
To inspectors
  • Each finished job adds to the inspector's pay
  • You mark the pay as sent; the inspector sees it in their portal
  • Agreements, instructions and 1099s are kept in Documents. 1099s are private: only you and that inspector can open them

6Keeping it safe

  • Every person has their own login and sees only their own information
  • Photos, documents and tax forms are stored privately, never as public links
  • Card details are never shown in full, only the last 4 digits
  • Clients never see a report until your office has approved it
