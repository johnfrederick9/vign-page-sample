FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do you provide services?

Nationwide across the USA, through our network of local inspectors and mystery shoppers.

How do I send you inspections?

Clients can import a list of assignments through the client portal, or send them to our staff and we will enter them for you.

How does the grading work?

Each item on the form is rated on a standard scale (1 = Good to 4 = Urgent). The system combines the ratings into an overall numerical score for the report.

How do I see my results?

Log in to the client portal to see each assignment's status, scores, findings and photos, and download the report.

Does it cost anything to join as an inspector or shopper?

No. Membership in the VIGN network is free. Sign up, tell us your coverage area, and receive assignments near you.

Do inspectors enter the property?

Only when the client authorizes it. Many inspections are exterior only. Inspectors record what they see, and never touch locks, utilities, belongings or occupants without the client's written permission.
