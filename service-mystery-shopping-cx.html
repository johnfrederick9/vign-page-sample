What we check

A standard checklist, every time

Each item is rated on the VIGN 1–4 scale (Good, Minor, Repair, Urgent) with findings and a photo. The system adds up the overall score automatically.

Best for: Brands with multiple locations that want consistent service everywhere.

  • Arrival, parking and entrance
  • Greeting and courtesy
  • Needs assessment and recommendations
  • Product or service presentation
  • Facility condition and cleanliness
  • Follow-up and farewell
  • Location-to-location score comparison
  • Repeat visits to track improvement
